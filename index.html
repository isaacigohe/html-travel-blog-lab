Adventures in Fire Island

Visiting Fire Island National Seashore was an unforgettable coastal escape filled with pristine sandy beaches, peaceful maritime forests, and scenic boardwalk trails. One of the biggest highlights of the trip was climbing the historic Fire Island Lighthouse for panoramic views of the Atlantic Ocean and Great South Bay, followed by spotting white-tailed deer wandering quietly near the Sunken Forest.
